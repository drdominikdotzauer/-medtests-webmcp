01 · Tools

One small, auditable agent surface

medtests_list_screeningsFind options from the user’s stated concern without inferring a diagnosis.
medtests_get_screeningReturn exact questions and permitted answer values.
medtests_score_screeningScore complete answers only after explicit consent, without retaining them.
medtests_get_crisis_resourcesStop the questionnaire flow and return crisis contacts.
medtests_get_chat_analysis_protocolAsk which ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Gemini, or other chats may be inspected before exploring symptom patterns.

04 · Contract

Private by construction

  • Screening indication only, never a diagnosis.
  • Explicit consent required before scoring.
  • Answers are not stored or returned.
  • Crisis signals take precedence over completion.
  • No accounts, analytics profiles, or advertising data.
